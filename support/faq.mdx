This page answers the questions our users ask most often. If you can't find what you're looking for here, reach out to the support team at [support@piixan.ai](mailto:support@piixan.ai) and a human will get back to you promptly.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What AI models does Piixan AI support?">
    Piixan AI currently offers three models, each optimised for different use cases:

    | Model | Description |
    |---|---|
    | `piixan-1` | A well-rounded model suited for everyday tasks — content generation, summarisation, classification, and general Q&A. |
    | `piixan-1-turbo` | A speed-optimised variant of `piixan-1` built for high-throughput workloads where latency matters more than maximum accuracy. |
    | `piixan-pro` | The most capable model in the lineup, designed for complex reasoning, long-context document analysis, and nuanced generation tasks. |

    Specify your chosen model in the `model` field of any API request, or set a workspace-level default in [Settings → General](/configuration/settings).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How are credits calculated?">
    Credits are consumed on a per-token basis. Every word — more precisely, every ~4 characters — in your prompt and in the model's response counts as approximately one token. Larger models like `piixan-pro` cost more credits per token than faster models like `piixan-1-turbo`.

    For a full breakdown of per-model token pricing and example cost calculations, see the [Credits & Usage](/concepts/credits-usage) page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do credits expire?">
    Credits purchased as part of a one-time credit pack are valid for **12 months from the date of purchase**. After 12 months, any unused purchased credits expire automatically.

    Monthly credits included in a paid plan (Growth or Scale) expire at the end of each billing cycle and do not roll over to the following month.

    Free trial credits (100 credits on the Free plan) also expire 12 months after your account is created.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my data used to train AI models?">
    No. Piixan AI never uses your prompts, completions, uploaded files, or any other user-generated content to train or fine-tune AI models — ours or third-party ones. Your data belongs to you.

    Request logs are retained for 30 days for debugging and support purposes, after which they are permanently deleted. You can disable request logging entirely in [Settings → Privacy](/configuration/settings#data--privacy) if your compliance requirements demand it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I run out of credits?">
    When your credit balance reaches zero, the Piixan AI API returns a **`402 Payment Required`** response for any request that would consume credits. Existing saved workflows will also stop executing until your balance is replenished.

    To restore access immediately, purchase additional credits from [Settings → Billing → Buy Credits](/configuration/billing#purchasing-credits). Credits are applied to your account as soon as payment is confirmed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Piixan AI for free?">
    Yes. When you create a new account, Piixan AI automatically credits your workspace with **100 free trial credits** — no credit card required. These credits let you explore the API, run your first workflows, and evaluate the platform before committing to a paid plan.

    Once your trial credits are exhausted, you need to upgrade to a paid plan or purchase a credit pack to continue using the platform. See the [Billing](/configuration/billing) page for plan details and pricing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my subscription?">
    You can cancel your subscription at any time without contacting support:

    1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Plans** in your dashboard.
    2. Click **Cancel Plan** beneath your current plan details.
    3. Confirm the cancellation in the dialog that appears.

    Your plan remains active until the end of the current billing cycle. After that, your workspace reverts to the Free tier. Your data, workflows, and settings are preserved — only credit allocations and rate limits change.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What file formats can I upload?">
    The `POST /v1/files/upload` endpoint accepts the following file formats:

    | Type | Extensions |
    |---|---|
    | Documents | `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.txt` |
    | Data | `.csv` |
    | Images | `.png`, `.jpg` / `.jpeg` |

    The maximum file size is **20 MB** per upload. Files larger than 20 MB must be split before uploading. Once uploaded, files are available for use in workflow steps and completion requests for up to 30 days.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a rate limit on API requests?">
    Yes, every plan has request rate limits to ensure fair usage and platform stability. Rate limits are enforced per API key, per minute. When you exceed your limit, the API returns a `429 Too Many Requests` response.

    For the full rate limit table by plan, retry guidance, and header documentation, see the [Rate Limits](/api-reference/rate-limits) reference page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    Email the Piixan AI support team at [support@piixan.ai](mailto:support@piixan.ai). Include as much context as possible in your message:

    - A clear description of the problem
    - The `X-Request-ID` header value from any failed API response
    - The workflow ID if the issue relates to a specific workflow run
    - Steps to reproduce the issue

    Growth and Scale plan subscribers receive a response within 48 and 24 hours respectively. Enterprise customers are served by a dedicated account manager.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
