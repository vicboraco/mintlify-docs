## Get Up and Running

Follow these three steps to go from a brand-new account to a working AI integration.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Visit [app.piixan.ai](https://app.piixan.ai) and sign up for a free account. Verify your email address to activate full platform access and claim your trial credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Your API Key">
    Open the [API Keys settings page](https://app.piixan.ai/settings/api-keys) in your dashboard. Click **Create New Key**, give it a descriptive name, and copy the generated key to a safe location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make Your First Request">
    Call the `/v1/completions` endpoint with your API key in the `Authorization` header. Within seconds you'll receive a fully formed AI response — ready to use in your product or workflow.
  </Step>
</Steps>
