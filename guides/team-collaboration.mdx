Piixan AI is designed for teams, not just individuals. A shared workspace lets everyone on your team create and run workflows, view AI outputs, and build on each other's work — all under a single account with centralized billing. Role-based access control means you decide exactly what each teammate can see and do, so you can give your whole organization access without worrying about accidental changes or unexpected costs.

## Inviting Team Members

<Steps>

  <Step title="Open Team Settings">
    Click your workspace name in the top-left corner of the dashboard, then select **Settings**. In the Settings sidebar, click **Team** to open the member management page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start the Invitation">
    Click **Invite Member** in the top-right corner of the Team page. A dialog box opens prompting you for the new member's details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the Email Address">
    Type the email address of the person you want to invite. You can invite multiple people at once by entering each address on a new line.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select a Role">
    Choose the appropriate role from the dropdown — **Admin**, **Editor**, or **Viewer**. See the [Roles and Permissions](#roles-and-permissions) section below if you are unsure which role to assign.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send the Invite">
    Click **Send Invite**. The recipient will receive an email with a secure link to join your workspace. The invitation expires after 7 days; you can resend it from the **Pending Invites** tab if needed.
  </Step>

</Steps>

## Roles and Permissions

Each role grants a specific set of permissions. Assign the most restrictive role that still lets the person do their job.

| Role | Invite Members | Create Workflows | Run Workflows | View Usage | Billing |
|------|:--------------:|:----------------:|:-------------:|:----------:|:-------:|
| Admin | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Editor | ✗ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ |
| Viewer | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ | ✗ |

- **Admin** — full control over the workspace, including billing and member management. Grant this role only to people who need to manage the account itself.
- **Editor** — can build and run workflows and view usage data, but cannot invite new members or access billing. This is the right role for most contributors.
- **Viewer** — read-only access to usage data. Useful for stakeholders who need visibility into AI consumption without the ability to make changes.

## Sharing Workflows

Every workflow you create lives inside your workspace and is visible to all members by default. You can change a workflow's visibility at any time from its settings panel.

- **Workspace** (default) — all members with Editor or Admin roles can view, duplicate, and run the workflow. Admins can also edit or delete it.
- **Private** — only you and workspace Admins can see or access the workflow. Use this setting while a workflow is still a draft or contains sensitive prompt logic you are not ready to share.

To change visibility, open the workflow, click the **⋯** menu in the top-right corner, select **Settings**, and toggle the **Visibility** option. Changes take effect immediately.

## Activity Log

The Activity Log gives you a full audit trail of every action taken in your workspace — who ran which workflow, when they ran it, what inputs they provided, and whether it succeeded or failed.

To view the log, go to **Settings** → **Activity**. You can filter entries by member, workflow, event type, or date range. Use the log to:

- Debug unexpected outputs by reviewing exactly what inputs were passed.
- Monitor usage patterns and identify which workflows are run most frequently.
- Audit access during security reviews or compliance checks.

Activity log entries are retained for 90 days on standard plans and 12 months on Enterprise plans.

<Note>
  Team plans are billed per seat — each active member counts toward your monthly seat total. Pending invitations do not consume a seat until the recipient accepts. If you need to free up a seat, you can deactivate a member from the **Team** settings page, which immediately revokes their access without deleting their workflows.
</Note>
