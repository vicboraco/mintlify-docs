Setting up your Piixan AI account takes just a few minutes. You'll create a workspace, invite your team, and configure your preferences so everyone can start automating workflows and generating AI-powered content right away.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up">
    Navigate to [https://app.piixan.ai/signup](https://app.piixan.ai/signup) and enter your full name, email address, and a strong password. After submitting the form, check your inbox for a verification email and click the confirmation link to activate your account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a Workspace">
    Once your email is verified, you'll be prompted to name your workspace. Choose a name that reflects your organization or project — for example, `Acme Corp` or `Marketing Team`. Select a plan that fits your needs; all new accounts start with a free trial so you can explore every feature before committing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite Team Members">
    Open **Settings → Team** from the left sidebar. Enter each teammate's email address and assign them a role — **Admin**, **Editor**, or **Viewer** — then click **Send Invites**. Each invitee will receive an email with a link to join your workspace. You can invite as many members as your plan allows and update roles at any time.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Workspace Settings">
    Head to **Settings → General** to fine-tune your workspace. Set a display name that appears across the dashboard, choose the timezone used for scheduling and logs, and select the default AI model that all new workflows will use unless overridden. Save your changes to apply them workspace-wide.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Account Roles

Piixan AI uses a three-tier role system to control what each team member can see and do inside a workspace. Assign roles carefully — you can always adjust them later from **Settings → Team**.

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| **Admin** | Full access to all workspace features, including billing, team management, API keys, and all content. |
| **Editor** | Can create, edit, run, and delete workflows and content, but cannot manage billing or team members. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access — can view workflows, results, and usage reports, but cannot make any changes. |

## Profile Settings

Your personal profile is separate from workspace settings and applies across every workspace you belong to. To update it, click your avatar in the top-right corner and select **Profile**.

- **Display Name** — The name shown to teammates in comments, activity logs, and shared views.
- **Avatar** — Upload a profile photo (PNG or JPG, up to 2 MB) to personalize your account.
- **Notification Preferences** — Choose which events trigger email or in-app notifications, such as workflow completions, team invitations, and usage alerts. You can toggle individual notification types or silence them all at once.

<Tip>
  Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) to add an extra layer of security to your account. Go to **Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication** and follow the prompts to link an authenticator app. Once enabled, you'll need to enter a one-time code every time you sign in from a new device.
</Tip>
