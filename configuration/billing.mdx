Piixan AI's billing portal gives you a single place to manage every financial aspect of your account — from choosing a plan and purchasing credit top-ups to downloading invoices and updating your payment method. All billing is handled securely through Stripe, so your card details are never stored on Piixan AI servers.

## Purchasing Credits

If you need more credits outside your plan's monthly allocation, you can buy a credit pack at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Buy Credits Page">
    Navigate to **Settings → Billing → Buy Credits** in your Piixan AI dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Credit Pack">
    Choose the pack size that matches your needs. Larger packs have a lower cost per credit. The available packs are displayed with their total credit value and price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Payment Information">
    Enter your card details in the Stripe-hosted payment form. If you already have a saved payment method on file, you can select it directly without re-entering card details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Purchase">
    Click **Purchase Credits**. Credits are added to your balance immediately upon successful payment — no waiting period required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Payment Methods

To add, remove, or update your saved credit cards, go to **Settings → Billing → Payment Methods**.

- **Add a card:** Click **Add Payment Method**, enter your card details in the Stripe form, and click **Save**.
- **Set a default card:** Click the three-dot menu next to any saved card and select **Set as Default**. The default card is charged automatically for subscription renewals and credit pack purchases.
- **Remove a card:** Click the three-dot menu next to the card and select **Remove**. You cannot remove your only saved payment method if you have an active paid subscription.

## Viewing Invoices

Every payment Piixan AI processes generates a downloadable invoice. Navigate to **Settings → Billing → Invoices** to see your full payment history.

Each invoice row shows the date, amount, and payment status. Click **Download PDF** on any row to save a formatted receipt suitable for expense reporting or accounting records.

## Upgrading or Downgrading Your Plan

To change your plan, go to **Settings → Billing → Plans** and click **Change Plan**.

- **Upgrades** take effect immediately. You are billed a prorated amount for the remainder of your current billing cycle, and the new credit allocation is added to your balance right away.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the end of your current billing cycle. You keep the features and credits of your current plan until then, and the lower-tier plan activates on your next renewal date.
